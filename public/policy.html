管理团队可以在不提前通知、不事先警告和不给予整改机会的情况下实施封禁。

警告、禁言、踢出房间、限制匹配、限制聊天或限制部分服务器功能，不属于本守则规定的正式处罚方式。

The only formal enforcement action imposed by the Niko Private Server for violations of these Rules is a ban.

Bans may be:

1. Temporary bans.

2. Permanent bans.

The administration team may determine the duration of a ban based on the nature and severity of the violation, its impact, the player’s previous violation history, the player’s cooperation with the investigation, and other relevant circumstances.

Any confirmed form of cheating will result in a ban.

Serious cheating, repeated violations, attacks against the server, malicious exploitation of vulnerabilities, disclosure of another person’s personal information, fabrication of evidence, threats against administrators, organization of violations, or ban evasion may result in an immediate permanent ban.

The administration team may impose a ban without prior notice, prior warning, or an opportunity to correct the conduct.

Warnings, chat restrictions, lobby removals, matchmaking restrictions, or restrictions on particular server functions are not formal enforcement actions under these Rules.

6. 请求、指导或安排他人代替被封禁玩家进入服务器。

7. 通过其他未经允许的方式绕过服务器封禁系统。

规避封禁属于独立违规行为。相关账号可以被封禁，原有临时封禁可以被延长或变更为永久封禁。

After being banned, a player must not evade, bypass, or weaken the effect of the ban by any means.

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

1. Creating or using another account to access the server.

2. Borrowing, purchasing, renting, or using another person’s account.

3. Allowing another person to use the banned player’s account.

4. Modifying, falsifying, or concealing device information, network information, identity information, or account association information.

5. Using a proxy, VPN, virtual machine, or other technical means to conceal associations between accounts.

6. Asking, instructing, or arranging for another person to access the server on behalf of the banned player.

7. Bypassing the server’s ban system through any other unauthorized method.

Ban evasion is a separate violation. Associated accounts may be banned, and an existing temporary ban may be extended or converted into a permanent ban.

第8节　封禁申诉
Section 8　Ban Appeals

如果你认为封禁存在错误，可以通过 Niko 私服公布的官方 Discord、官方 QQ 群或官方电子邮件提交申诉。

申诉必须在你首次看到封禁提示之日起七个自然日内提交。

七个自然日的申诉期限从玩家首次看到或按照正常情况应当看到封禁提示之时开始计算。

超过七个自然日提交的申诉将被无条件拒绝。管理团队主动决定重新审查的情况除外。

申诉应当至少包含以下内容：

1. 被封禁账号、玩家名称、玩家编号或其他可以识别账号的信息。

2. 封禁提示的完整截图。

3. 首次看到封禁提示的日期和时间。

4. 对相关事件的完整说明。

5. 认为封禁存在错误的具体理由。

6. 可以支持申诉的截图、录像、日志或其他证据。

7. 管理团队要求提供的其他必要信息。

出现下列情况之一的，申诉可以被直接拒绝：

1. 超过七个自然日的申诉期限。

2. 重复提交相同或实质相同的申诉。

3. 提供虚假信息、伪造证据或故意隐瞒重要事实。

4. 辱骂、威胁、骚扰或恶意攻击审核人员。

5. 拒绝提供识别账号或核实事件所必需的信息。

6. 通过非官方渠道反复联系或骚扰工作人员。

7. 由与封禁事件无关的第三方代替被封禁玩家提出申诉。

8. 在申诉期间继续规避封禁或实施其他违规行为。

提交申诉不代表封禁一定会被撤销，也不会自动暂停封禁的执行。

管理团队在审核申诉后，可以维持、缩短、延长、变更为永久封禁或撤销原封禁。

原则上，每次封禁仅接受一次正式申诉。申诉审核结果通常为最终结果。

If you believe that a ban was issued in error, you may submit an appeal through the official Discord, official QQ group, or official email address published by the Niko Private Server.

An appeal must be submitted within seven calendar days from the date on which you first see the ban notice.

The seven-calendar-day appeal period begins when the player first sees, or under normal circumstances should have seen, the ban notice.

Appeals submitted after seven calendar days will be rejected without exception, unless the administration team independently decides to reopen the case.

An appeal must include at least the following information:

1. The banned account, player name, player identifier, or other information sufficient to identify the account.

2. A complete screenshot of the ban notice.

3. The date and time when the ban notice was first seen.

4. A complete explanation of the relevant events.

5. The specific reasons why the player believes the ban was incorrect.

6. Supporting screenshots, video recordings, logs, or other evidence.

7. Any other necessary information requested by the administration team.

An appeal may be rejected immediately under any of the following circumstances:

1. It is submitted after the seven-calendar-day deadline.

2. The same or substantially identical appeal is repeatedly submitted.

3. False information or fabricated evidence is provided, or material facts are intentionally concealed.

4. Review staff are insulted, threatened, harassed, or maliciously attacked.

5. Information necessary to identify the account or verify the incident is withheld.

6. Staff members are repeatedly contacted or harassed through unofficial channels.

7. An unrelated third party submits the appeal on behalf of the banned player.

8. The player continues to evade the ban or commits additional violations while the appeal is under review.

Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that the ban will be removed and does not automatically suspend enforcement of the ban.

After reviewing an appeal, the administration team may uphold, reduce, extend, convert to a permanent ban, or revoke the original ban.

As a general rule, only one formal appeal will be accepted for each ban. The appeal decision will normally be final.
